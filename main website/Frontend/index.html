Core Advantages

Designed around hardware you already own

Why buy expensive commercial cloud subscriptions or complex NAS hardware when your old phone has gigabytes of reliable flash storage?

Zero-Trust Network Isolation

Direct remote access without opening a single router port.

Traditional home servers and NAS drives require manual router port forwarding, opening inbound holes in your private Wi-Fi firewall. ZdexCloud reverses the flow: your Android phone initiates an outbound encrypted WebSocket relay tunnel directly to our gateway.

No Port Forwarding: Works behind symmetric NAT, carrier CGNAT, and mobile 4G/5G.
Zero Router Vulnerability: Your home network has zero listening ports exposed to the Internet.
Local Hardware Privacy: File bytes stream directly from phone storage to your browser.
NETWORK ARCHITECTURE COMPARISON TLS 1.3
Traditional Home NAS & Cloud Storage Requires open router ports (80/443/5000), dynamic DNS configuration, high standby power (35W–90W), and recurring subscriptions.
ZdexCloud Android Phone Server Zero router ports opened. Phone initiates outbound TLS 1.3 WebSocket tunnel. 100% on-device storage with 2W–5W micro-power efficiency.

Privacy & Security Principles

Designed around data privacy and local ownership

We believe your personal documents, photos, and records belong on physical hardware under your control.

Local Storage Ownership: Your files reside on your phone's storage media. The central cloud plane holds metadata and endpoint mapping only.
Outbound Secure Architecture: Android servers connect outbound to our gateway, removing the risk of open router ports and exposed home networks.
Strict Credential Separation: Platform authentication manages device registration; dedicated server credentials authorize file operations.
