The 5-Stage Journey to Private Storage

How your spare Android device converts into an always-on, zero-port personal file server.

01

Install the ZdexCloud Android App

Download and install the official ZdexCloud APK onto your dedicated smartphone host. Compatible with any device running Android 8.0 (Oreo) or higher, including legacy phones with as little as 1.5 GB of RAM.

Hardware requirement: Android 8.0+ • Minimal RAM footprint
02

Sign In and Configure Your Device

Sign in with your verified platform account. The app registers the phone with your central device registry (linking it as one of your up to 5 allowed server hosts) and prompts you to configure dedicated file-server credentials.

Dual Auth: Web access password is fully isolated from master account
03

Your Phone Becomes the Storage Host

Switch on Server Mode. The app initiates its embedded lightweight HTTP file server engine directly on the phone. All files remain strictly on your phone's internal storage or inserted microSD card.

100% Local Media: Central servers never store or inspect your files
04

The Device Establishes the Outbound Secure Connection

Your phone initiates an outbound encrypted WebSocket tunnel (TLS 1.3) directly to the ZdexCloud gateway. Because the connection is outbound, it traverses NAT, carrier CGNAT, and mobile firewalls with zero router port forwarding.

Zero Port Forwarding: No open home ports • Safe behind symmetric NAT
05

Access Your Files Remotely

Open your unique server subdomain URL in any web browser. Authenticate with your dedicated server credentials to browse folders, upload documents, download backups, and stream photos and videos anywhere in the world.

Accessible everywhere: Desktop, laptop, tablet, or phone web browsers
Reliability Engineering

Three tips for 24/7 uninterrupted hosting

Keep your Android phone server stable, connected, and operating efficiently.

Battery Optimization Whitelist

Android aggressively suspends background services to save battery. Add ZdexCloud to the "Do Not Optimize" battery whitelist in Android Settings so Server Mode runs continuously.

Stable Wi-Fi & Power Supply

Keep your phone connected to a standard 5W/10W charger and dedicated home Wi-Fi. The phone's built-in battery functions as an uninterrupted power supply (UPS) during short power outages.

Storage Permissions

Ensure "All Files Access" is granted to the app so the embedded file manager can browse your phone storage, camera rolls, download folders, and external SD cards without restriction.

Privacy & Sovereignty

What happens to your files?

Your files remain stored directly on your Android phone's flash storage media. The platform provides software control plane metadata and outbound routing tunnels — it does not act as your central file repository.

Direct data path: Your phone's storage media streams data directly to your browser session.
