Complete User Journey

From Registration to Remote File Access

Follow the 10-step sequence that sets up your phone as an accessible personal server without complex router tweaking.

The 10-Step Setup & Remote Sequence

STEP 01

Create Platform Account

Register your user account on the main website using your email address.

STEP 02

Verify Email Address

Confirm email ownership to secure device management permissions.

STEP 03

Install Android App

Download and install the Flutter Android application on your dedicated phone.

STEP 04

Sign Into App

Authenticate inside the Android app using your platform account credentials.

STEP 05

Register Android Device

Link the physical Android phone with your platform account registry.

STEP 06

Configure File Server Credentials

Create dedicated username and password credentials specifically for web file access.

STEP 07

Start Server Mode

Enable Server Mode on the Android phone to initiate the local file-managing service.

STEP 08

Secure Outbound Tunnel Connection

The app establishes an outbound connection to our gateway without open router ports.

STEP 09

Remote Endpoint Discovery

Open the main website from any browser, select your online device, and launch your assigned endpoint.

STEP 10

Authenticate & Manage Files

Log in with your file-server credentials to browse, upload, download, and organize files stored on your phone.

What happens to your files?

Your files remain stored directly on your Android phone's flash storage media. The platform provides software control plane metadata and outbound routing tunnels — it does not act as your central file repository.
