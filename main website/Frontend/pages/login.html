Platform Authentication & Sessions

Answers to common questions regarding account authentication, 24-hour session expiration, and server continuity.

ZdexCloud enforces a strict 24-hour absolute session lifetime for browser dashboard logins. Exactly 24 hours after signing in, the authentication session expires automatically to prevent unauthorized access from abandoned browser sessions. Continued browsing activity does not extend the session indefinitely.

When your session reaches the 24-hour limit, the web dashboard prompts you to sign in again. Your personal files and registered server status remain completely unaffected; you simply re-enter your credentials to resume browser access.

No. The Android phone server operates completely independently of whether you are logged into the website. The phone runs its own background service 24/7. Signing out of the website or session expiration does not stop or pause your Android server.

You will need to sign in again if: 1) Your strict 24-hour session expired; 2) You explicitly clicked Sign Out; 3) You cleared your browser cookies or local storage; or 4) You updated your password.
