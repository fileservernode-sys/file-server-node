Upgrade to Pro Yearly

Upgrade your active monthly subscription to annual billing to lock in discounted yearly pricing and continuous coverage.

Current Plan: ZdexCloud Pro Monthly
Target Plan: ZdexCloud Pro Yearly
Proration Credit: Automatic Unused Days Credit
Any unused days remaining on your active monthly billing period are calculated authoritatively by the billing engine and credited directly against the annual invoice.

Switch to Monthly Billing

Schedule your subscription to switch to Pro Monthly billing at the conclusion of your current annual period.

Current Plan: ZdexCloud Pro Yearly
Target Plan: ZdexCloud Pro Monthly
Effective Date: End of paid annual period
You will retain full Pro Yearly access and 5 server slots until the effective date. No mid-term refund is issued for the current period. Future renewals will bill at the monthly rate.

Keep Annual Plan

This will cancel your scheduled switch to monthly billing and restore automatic annual renewal for your ZdexCloud Pro Yearly subscription.

Cancel Pro Subscription

Why are you cancelling?

Your feedback directly improves our personal file server platform.

Impact of Cancellation

  • Your Pro features remain active until the end of your current paid billing period.
  • After expiration, your account reverts to the Free Tier (1 active server slot).
  • Your Android phone files and host storage are never deleted.
