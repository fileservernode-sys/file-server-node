Legal & Privacy Framework

Privacy Policy Framework

Last updated: August 15, 2026. This policy outlines how data is handled across our main website, control plane, and local file storage platform.

Notice: This Privacy Policy outlines our architectural data handling principles. Final legal wording is subject to formal legal counsel review prior to commercial deployment.

1. Introduction

RemoteNode ("the Platform") is designed around local-first storage principles. We prioritize user privacy by allowing personal files to remain on physical hardware owned by the user.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information: When registering, we collect your email address and password hash for account authentication.

Device Information: We store device identifiers, device nicknames, online/offline status, and assigned gateway routing tokens to facilitate server discovery.

3. File & Personal Data Principles

The platform control plane does not act as a central cloud storage host for user files. User documents, media, and folders remain stored on the Android device.

4. Security

We implement standard security measures including password hashing, TLS encryption for control plane traffic, and separate authentication layers for file-server access.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this privacy framework, contact us via our Contact Page.
