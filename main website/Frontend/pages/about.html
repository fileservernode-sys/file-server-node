Our Purpose

Giving Unused Hardware a Second Life

Hundreds of millions of old smartphones sit unused in drawers despite containing fast flash storage, low-power ARM processors, and built-in battery backups.

The Product Mission

Our goal is to make personal self-hosted cloud storage accessible to non-technical users by abstracting away port forwarding, dynamic DNS, TLS certificates, and complex server administration.

By combining hardware you already own with simple software control planes and secure outbound tunneling, we enable private personal cloud storage without monthly storage rental fees.

Core Principles

  • ♻ Sustainable Tech Reuse: Repurpose existing smartphones into energy-efficient 24/7 personal servers.
  • 🔒 Local Data Ownership: Your files remain stored physically on your Android device hardware.
  • ⚡ Frictionless Simplicity: Zero manual router port forwarding or public IP management.
