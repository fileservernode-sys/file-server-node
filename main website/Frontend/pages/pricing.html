How ZdexCloud Pricing Works: Storage vs. Server Slots

Unlike conventional cloud platforms that charge recurring monthly fees per gigabyte, ZdexCloud charges ₹0 for storage capacity. Your storage is provided by your phone's internal flash storage or microSD card (whether 64GB, 256GB, or 1TB). Our commercial plans define how many registered Android server nodes you can manage under one account (1 on Free, up to 5 on Pro) and provide priority gateway relay routing.

Long-Term Economics

ZdexCloud vs. Traditional Subscription Cloud Storage

Compare the long-term total cost of ownership (TCO) of renting centralized cloud storage vs. hosting on Android hardware you already own.

Traditional Cloud Subscriptions

Commercial cloud providers (Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, Dropbox, iCloud) charge recurring monthly or annual rents for proprietary storage blocks (100GB, 200GB, 2TB).

  • Monthly Cost: ₹130 to ₹650+ per month depending on tier
  • 1-Year Cost: ₹1,560 to ₹7,800+ per year
  • 3-Year Cost: ₹4,680 to ₹23,400+ paid indefinitely
  • Data Custody: Files stored on corporate multi-tenant data centers
  • Price Stability: Vulnerable to sudden subscription fee increases
Cancel subscription = lose immediate access to your stored files unless transferred.

ZdexCloud Personal File Server

Repurposes existing Android hardware into private servers. Zero per-gigabyte storage rent. You control your storage media directly.

  • Free Tier (1 Server): ₹0 forever for 128GB–512GB+ on phone
  • Pro Monthly (Up to 5 Servers): ₹49/month with full flexibility
  • Pro Yearly (Up to 5 Servers): ₹500/year (effective ₹41.67/month, Save ₹88/yr)
  • 3-Year Total Cost: ₹0 (Free) or ₹1,500 total (Pro Yearly for 5 phones)
  • Data Custody: Physical hardware hosted on your device; no permanent central file storage
Cancel subscription = your files remain completely safe and accessible on your physical Android phone.

Honest Hardware & Operational Trade-Offs

We believe in complete technical transparency. While commercial cloud services factor massive data center operating costs into your monthly bill, ZdexCloud shifts hosting to your physical premises:

Hardware Ownership

You provide the spare Android smartphone (Android 8.0+), charger, and optional microSD card. You never pay hardware rental fees to ZdexCloud.

Electricity Footprint

An idle Android phone server consumes approximately 2W to 4W of power (~₹20 to ₹50 in electricity per year), which is vastly cheaper than cloud subscriptions or traditional 50W PC NAS setups.

Home Network Bandwidth

File download speed depends on the phone's home Wi-Fi or cellular upload bandwidth. For high-bandwidth 4K video streaming, ensure your phone has a stable Wi-Fi connection.
