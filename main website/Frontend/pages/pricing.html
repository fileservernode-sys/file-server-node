Got Questions?

Pricing Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, transparent answers regarding billing, server limits, regional currency, and subscription policies.

The Free plan allows you to register and remotely access 1 Android phone file server with zero storage hosting fees. It includes full access to the browser-based web file manager, secure outbound TLS 1.3 relay tunneling, media streaming, and standard 24-hour web session security.

Both Pro Monthly and Pro Yearly support up to 5 simultaneously registered Android server nodes under a single account. All 5 nodes can be managed, switched, and accessed from your centralized web dashboard with priority gateway relay routing.

Both plans grant the exact same technical capabilities (up to 5 servers and priority relay). Pro Monthly offers month-to-month flexibility, while Pro Yearly provides substantial annual savings and locks in your plan price for 12 months.

In India, Pro Yearly costs ₹500/year compared to ₹49/month (₹588/year equivalent), saving ₹88/year (an effective monthly rate of ₹41.67/month). Internationally, Pro Yearly costs $9.99/year compared to $0.99/month ($11.88/year equivalent), saving $1.89/year (an effective monthly rate of $0.83/month).

Your authoritative billing currency is determined exclusively by your confirmed billing country. Accounts with a confirmed billing country of India are billed in Indian Rupees (INR). Accounts with confirmed billing countries outside India are billed in US Dollars (USD).

No. IP-based geolocation is used only as a non-authoritative storefront presentation hint. Your official billing country and billing currency are only established when explicitly confirmed by you in your account settings or at checkout.

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time. Upon cancellation, your paid entitlements remain fully active until the end of your current paid billing period without premature cutoff.

Your data is never deleted because it stays on your physical phone. If Pro expires, your account transitions safely to the Free tier (1 active server limit). No servers or files are destroyed; your first paired device remains active, and additional slots become paused until Pro is reactivated.
