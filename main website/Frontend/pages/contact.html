Support Answers

Frequently Asked Support Questions

Direct guidance on contacting support, troubleshooting offline phones, and protecting account credentials.

How do I contact ZdexCloud support?

You can contact ZdexCloud support by submitting the contact form on this page or by emailing support@zdexcloud.com directly.

Our technical engineering team reviews inquiries regarding account authentication, Android server setup, and gateway connectivity.

What information should I include in a support request?

Please include your platform account email, the Android phone model and OS version, your web browser and operating system, the status displayed in the app, and the exact text of any error message.

Providing specific diagnostic context allows our team to quickly identify whether the problem stems from OEM battery killing, Wi-Fi sleep policies, or network routing.

Can I contact ZdexCloud about a remote connection problem?

Yes, our team can help troubleshoot gateway relay connectivity, TLS handshake failures, and device registration issues.

Before reaching out, verify that the Android phone is connected to active Wi-Fi, battery optimization is disabled for the app, and the Android foreground service is actively running.

What should I do if my Android server is offline?

Check that your phone has continuous power, is connected to Wi-Fi, and that Android has not killed the background foreground service.

On aggressive Android OEM skins (such as Xiaomi MIUI, Samsung One UI, or OnePlus OxygenOS), you must explicitly set battery optimization to "Unrestricted" and lock the app in the recent apps tray to support continuous background operation.

Should I send my password or OTP to support?

No, never send your password, OTP code, access token, or on-device file-server credentials to support or anyone else.

ZdexCloud support engineers will never ask for your account password or your private on-device server credentials. If anyone claims to represent ZdexCloud and asks for your credentials, do not disclose them.
