User-Operated Host Hardware: Clarifying the Storage Model

You supply the physical storage hardware, and your files reside on your phone. ZdexCloud is not a centralized cloud storage provider, does not retain copies of your files, and does not maintain backup archives of your personal data.

2. Architecture & Service Boundaries

To ensure full clarity regarding rights and liabilities, the platform is divided into distinct operational boundaries:

The ZdexCloud Platform Service

Operates the central control plane, user account authentication, device registration database, pairing session brokering, and the TLS-secured outbound WebSocket relay gateway.

The User-Operated Host Node

Consists of the Android phone, the Android operating system, internal flash memory, microSD expansion storage, and local network connectivity managed entirely by you.

You retain sole and exclusive title, intellectual property rights, and physical custody of all documents, media, software, and files served from your Android device.

  • On-Device Custody: Because files never reside on ZdexCloud central disks, you maintain absolute sovereign custody of your physical storage media.

5. Network Connectivity & Zero Port Forwarding

Traditional home servers require configuring public IP addresses, router NAT traversal, dynamic DNS records, and opening router inbound ports. ZdexCloud eliminates these security risks through an outbound connection model:

  • Outbound-Only Encrypted Tunnel: The Android app initiates an outbound TLS connection over standard HTTPS/WSS port 443 to the ZdexCloud relay gateway.
  • Zero Port Forwarding: You are not required to open router ports, expose internal IP addresses to the public internet, or maintain dynamic DNS records.
  • Carrier-Grade NAT (CGNAT) Compatibility: This architecture allows the server to function seamlessly across mobile 4G/5G networks, public Wi-Fi, and residential ISPs with symmetric CGNAT.

6. Strict User Backup Responsibility & Data Loss Disclaimer

CRITICAL REQUIREMENT: Because ZdexCloud does not copy, duplicate, or store your files on central infrastructure, you must maintain independent 3-2-1 backups (three copies of data across two different media with one offsite copy).

Smartphones and microSD cards are single physical nodes subject to hardware loss, physical impact, flash memory wear, accidental erasure, or operating system failure. Under no circumstances is ZdexCloud liable for:

  • Loss, corruption, alteration, or unavailability of files stored on your Android hardware.

Direct Answers & Operational Policies

Frequently Asked Terms & Policy Questions

Clear, precise answers to common questions about ownership, on-device physical storage, backup obligations, and service boundaries.

What is ZdexCloud?

ZdexCloud is a personal file server platform that turns an Android smartphone into a private, self-hosted file server accessible from any web browser without requiring port forwarding, router configuration, or public IP addresses.

Who provides the storage?

You provide the physical storage. All files, documents, and media remain on the internal flash storage or microSD card of your Android phone. ZdexCloud provides only the control plane, pairing signaling, and secure relay gateway.

Do I need port forwarding?

No. ZdexCloud operates through an outbound-only encrypted WebSocket connection over standard HTTPS/WSS port 443, eliminating router port forwarding, DDNS, and firewall reconfiguration entirely.

How many servers can I register?

The Free plan includes 1 server instance. Upgrading to Pro Monthly (₹49/month) or Pro Yearly (₹500/year, with a saving of ₹88 annually) unlocks up to 5 concurrent server instances under a single account.

Is ZdexCloud responsible for backing up my files?

No. Because files remain strictly on your physical Android hardware and are not replicated on ZdexCloud servers, you are solely responsible for maintaining independent 3-2-1 backups of all critical data.

Can my Android server stop working because of battery optimization?

Yes. Aggressive OEM background task managers can terminate the foreground service if unrestricted battery settings are not granted. Users must configure the ZdexCloud Android application to run unrestricted in Android system settings.

What happens when my web session expires?

Web dashboard sessions expire strictly after 24 hours of creation for security. However, your Android server lifecycle is completely independent: the phone background service continues serving files uninterrupted, and you simply sign back into the web portal.

Can I use ZdexCloud for illegal or unauthorized activity?

No. Our Acceptable Use Policy strictly prohibits using the platform, relay infrastructure, or software for distributing malicious software, child sexual abuse material, copyright infringement, unauthorized network scanning, or abusive operations.
